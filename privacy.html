Privacy Policy

Slipper Storm (Terlik Yağmuru) · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Slipper Storm and what your rights are.

In short:

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't run any servers. The game sends no personal data to us.
  • The game is free. The current version (1.0) has no ads and sends no data to us or anyone else.
  • If ads are added later, they will be served by Google AdMob, which processes some device data to do so (see section 3). Ads will only be tailored to your interests if you allow it.

1. Who we are

Dodoca Studios is the studio name of Doğukan, an independent developer based in Türkiye. "We" in this policy means Doğukan. Contact: dodocompanytr@gmail.com

2. Data that stays on your device

The game stores the following only on your device: your best score, language and sound settings, your privacy choice, whether the tips have been shown, and counters that control how often ads appear. This data is never sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

This section will apply once ads are added to the app. The current version (1.0) has no ads.

In the mobile app, ads are served by Google AdMob. The browser version has no ads. To serve ads, Google may collect the following from your device:

  • Advertising ID: the Android advertising ID, or the IDFA on iPhone. On iPhone, the IDFA is only used if you allow tracking when Apple asks.
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device information: model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • Diagnostic and performance data from the ads library

This data is used to show ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow it, choose ads based on your interests.

Your choice: The game asks you the first time it opens. If you tap "Accept", ads may be tailored to your interests. If you tap "Only general ads", no personalisation is used. Even for general ads, Google processes some of the data above to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. Players in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent form instead.

You can change your decision at any time with the Privacy settings button in the game menu. You can also manage your advertising ID on iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and on Android in the Ads section of your device settings.

To learn how Google uses data, see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

A "Remove ads" purchase may be added to the game in the future. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your card details never reach us.

5. If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We keep this correspondence for up to 2 years after the matter is closed.

6. Legal bases

  • Personalised ads and the related transfer of data abroad: your consent.
  • Showing general ads, measurement, security and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free.
  • Replying to your email: handling your request and our legal obligations.

7. International transfers

Google processes data on servers outside your country, for example in the United States and Europe. For personalised ads, this transfer is based on your consent. Other transfers are covered by Google's privacy policy and its data transfer safeguards.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is subject to Google's own retention periods.

9. Children

Slipper Storm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's data is being processed, please contact us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can withdraw consent for personalised ads in the game's Privacy settings.

To make a request, email dodocompanytr@gmail.com. We will reply free of charge within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). For data processed by Google, you can also contact Google directly.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, and the date above will change. We will also announce important changes in the game.
